This release preserves the high-level sequence of acknowledging emotion, separating facts from interpretations, and offering actionable choices. It changes the product focus toward ordinary pursuit, chat, invitation, interest judgment, ambiguity, confession, and post-rejection handling. It does not require personality scoring or persistent relationship files, does not set gender-based initiative defaults, and does not include automatic chat reading, monitoring, or sending.

The user chooses the route. The Skill provides bounded methods for that route without promising success, manipulating the other person, or bypassing refusal. Safety guidance is loaded only when triggered by the facts.
